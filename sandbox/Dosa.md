# Masala Dosa with Coconut Chutney

## Masala Dosa Ingredients:
- 1 cup rice
- 1/2 cup urad dal (split black gram)
- 1/2 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
- Salt to taste
- Oil for cooking

### For Potato Filling:
- 3-4 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
- 1 medium onion, finely sliced
- 2 green chilies, chopped
- 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
- A few curry leaves
- 2 tablespoons oil
- Salt to taste
- Fresh coriander leaves, chopped

## Coconut Chutney Ingredients:
- 1 cup fresh grated coconut
- 2 tablespoons roasted chana dal (Bengal gram)
- 1-2 green chilies (adjust to taste)
- 1/2 inch piece ginger
- Salt to taste
- Water as needed

### For Tempering:
- 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
- 1 dried red chili
- A few curry leaves
- 1 tablespoon oil

---

## Preparation:

### Masala Dosa Batter:
1. Wash and soak rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds separately in water for 4-6 hours.
2. Grind urad dal and fenugreek seeds to a smooth batter adding water as needed.
3. Grind rice to a slightly coarse batter.
4. Mix both batters, add salt, and allow to ferment overnight or 8-12 hours.
5. The batter should rise and be slightly bubbly.

### Potato Filling:
1. Heat oil in a pan, add mustard seeds, let them splutter.
2. Add curry leaves, green chilies, and onions. Sauté till onions are translucent.
3. Add turmeric powder and mashed potatoes, mix well.
4. Add salt and coriander leaves. Cook for 5 mins and set aside.

### Coconut Chutney:
1. Grind coconut, roasted chana dal, green chilies, ginger, salt, and water to a smooth chutney.
2. For tempering, heat oil, add mustard seeds, dried red chili, and curry leaves. Pour over chutney.

---

## Cooking Dosa:
1. Heat a non-stick pan or cast iron tawa.
2. Pour a ladleful of batter in the center, spread in a circular motion to make a thin crepe.
3. Drizzle oil around the edges, cook till golden and crisp.
4. Place a portion of potato filling on one side, fold dosa over.
5. Serve hot with coconut chutney.

---

This classic masala dosa with coconut chutney is delicious for breakfast or any time snack.
